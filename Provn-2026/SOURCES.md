## Product and operating claims

The project documents define PROVN verification as checking **identity, trade/profession information, and consent**. This is not skills certification. Ratings are **Speed, Quality, and Behavior** and should come from real service interactions.

Launch districts, expansion sequence, revenue streams, pilot metrics, and B2B opportunities are planning assumptions unless supported by dated operational evidence. No current traction, revenue, partnerships, completed jobs, or active service coverage is established by the materials reviewed.
