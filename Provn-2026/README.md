# PROVN

**No More Asking Around for Technicians.**

PROVN is a referral-based technician network that turns personal recommendations into a searchable way to discover and evaluate service professionals. Customers can discover technicians introduced by people who know them, review identity and profession verification status, and compare real customer feedback across **Speed, Quality, and Behavior**.

## How it works

1. **Refer** — someone recommends a technician they personally know.
2. **Verify** — PROVN checks the technician's identity, trade/profession information, and consent to be listed and contacted.
3. **Discover** — customers search and compare relevant technicians.
4. **Service** — the customer and technician complete a real service interaction.
5. **Review** — the customer shares feedback across Speed, Quality, and Behavior.

Verification is not a technical skills certification and does not guarantee service quality. Reputation is built through real service experiences.

## Proposed launch direction

Initial Cairo districts: **Maadi, Nasr City, and Helwan**. Proposed expansion: **Greater Cairo → Giza → Alexandria**. This is a proposed rollout sequence, not a claim of current coverage or operations.

## Revenue model (phased)

1. Advertising, after sufficient user density and traffic.
2. Premium Emergency Tier, once trusted technician supply can reliably support urgent requests.
3. B2B Elite Contracts, as a longer-term opportunity for property managers and facilities-management organizations.

These are planned revenue streams, not current revenue claims.
