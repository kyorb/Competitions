# Marketing Plan

## Purpose

Introduce PROVN as a referral-based network that makes trusted technician discovery more organized, searchable, and transparent.

## Initial audiences

### Customers
- Households and individuals who need maintenance or repair services.
- People who currently ask friends, relatives, neighbors, or local communities for recommendations.

### Technicians
- Independent technicians who want to reach customers beyond their existing personal networks.
- Technicians willing to complete onboarding and verification steps.

### Potential B2B customers
- Property managers and facilities-management organizations with recurring maintenance needs. This segment should be validated through interviews before significant investment.

## Proposed launch geography

**Initial districts:** Maadi, Nasr City, and Helwan.  
**Expansion sequence:** Greater Cairo, then Giza and Alexandria.

This is a proposed rollout strategy, not a statement that PROVN currently operates in these areas.

## Marketing objectives for an initial pilot

- Recruit an initial pool of technicians through personal referrals.
- Test whether customers understand and trust the referral-first model.
- Generate real service requests in the selected pilot districts.
- Collect feedback on discovery, verification clarity, service experience, and reviews.
- Explore B2B needs through customer discovery interviews.

Avoid setting numerical targets until the team has agreed on capacity, budget, and a measurable pilot plan.

## Acquisition approach

### Customer acquisition
- Local community groups and neighborhood networks.
- Referral sharing by early users.
- Short educational content explaining how referrals and verification work.
- Demonstrations of the discovery and review experience.

### Technician acquisition
- Personal recommendations from people who know the technician.
- Direct outreach through local networks.
- Clear explanation of onboarding, consent, verification, and review expectations.

### B2B discovery
- Interview property managers and facilities-management teams.
- Understand response-time, coverage, quality-control, and invoicing requirements.
- Test a small pilot only after operational capacity is clear.

## Phased rollout

1. **Pilot:** concentrate on the selected Cairo districts and test the core referral-to-review loop.
2. **Improve:** use customer and technician feedback to improve onboarding, discovery, service requests, and review handling.
3. **Expand:** widen coverage only when local supply, demand, and service reliability justify it.

## Channels to test

- Facebook and Instagram.
- Local community groups.
- Search-friendly website content, when a website is available.
- WhatsApp for communication and community outreach, without treating it as the product's core service.
- Direct B2B outreach.
