# Branding & Marketing Execution

## 1. Brand foundation

**Brand name:** PROVN  
**Brand mantra:** No More Asking Around for Technicians.  
**Brand promise:** Trusted recommendations, clear verification, and real customer reputation.

The identity should feel dependable, practical, warm, and modern. It should communicate trust and local service—not industrial machinery or a generic technology platform.

## 2. color palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Dark Coral | `#C1502E` | Primary actions and key brand accents |
| Gold Luster | `#D5AC55` | Verification and reward accents |
| Mushroom Black | `#2B2420` | Text and dark surfaces |
| Cotton Lace | `#FBF9F4` | Backgrounds and light surfaces |

## 3. Logo direction

The logo concept combines:
- A hexagonal nut, representing practical trades and service work.
- A lens/magnifying-glass motif, representing discovery and verification.
- A small gold point as a restrained accent.

## 4. Typography

- **English headings:** Plus Jakarta Sans, SemiBold/Bold.
- **English body:** Inter.
- **Arabic:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic.

## 5. Voice and messaging

Use clear, practical language. Avoid claims that imply a guarantee or certification that PROVN does not provide.

Preferred language:
- “Identity and profession verified”
- “Recommended by someone who knows the technician”
- “Rated by real customers”
- “Build a reputation through completed services”

Avoid:
- “Certified technician” unless a separate, legitimate certification exists.
- “Guaranteed quality”
- “Fully vetted skills” when only identity and profession are checked.
- “The most reliable marketplace” as an unproven superlative.

## 6. Content pillars

| Pillar | Example topic | Format |
|---|---|---|
| How PROVN works | Explain Refer → Verify → Discover → Service → Review | Short video / carousel |
| Trust & verification | What identity and profession verification mean—and do not mean | Short video / FAQ |
| Customer education | How to compare Speed, Quality, and Behavior ratings | Carousel |
| Technician stories | Introduce a technician with consent and factual details | Profile / short video |
| Real service feedback | Share an approved, authentic customer review | Quote / story |

## 7. Example weekly publishing rhythm

- **Sunday:** How PROVN works or verification explainer.
- **Tuesday:** Practical maintenance education.
- **Thursday:** Technician story or authentic customer feedback, when available.
