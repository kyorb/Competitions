# Problem Statement

People often struggle to identify a reliable technician when they need maintenance or repair services. Recommendations are shared through personal contacts and informal conversations, but that information is scattered and difficult to compare.

At the same time, capable independent technicians can find it difficult to reach new customers and build a visible reputation beyond their existing personal networks.

**PROVN addresses this trust and discovery gap by organizing personal recommendations into a searchable technician network, supported by identity and profession verification and real customer reviews.**

## What the problem is — and is not

- The problem is not that reliable technicians do not exist.
- The problem is that trust-based recommendations are fragmented and hard to search, compare, and evaluate.
- PROVN does not claim to certify technical skill through identity checks. Service quality is reflected through real customer experiences.
