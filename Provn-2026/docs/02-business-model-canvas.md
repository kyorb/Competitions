# Business Model Canvas

**Brand mantra:** No More Asking Around for Technicians.

## Customer segments

- Individuals and households looking for maintenance and repair technicians.
- Organizations with recurring maintenance needs, including property managers and facilities-management companies.
- Independent technicians seeking access to customers and a way to build a credible service reputation.

## Value propositions

### For customers
- Discover technicians introduced through personal recommendations.
- See identity/profession verification status clearly.
- Compare real customer feedback across Speed, Quality, and Behavior.
- Find relevant technicians by trade, location, and availability.

### For technicians
- Gain discoverability beyond existing personal contacts.
- Build a reputation through real completed-service reviews.
- Access potential customer requests through a referral-led network.

### For B2B customers
- Potential access to a more organized pool of vetted technicians.
- A possible managed relationship and support model, subject to pilot validation.

## Channels

- PROVN mobile application.
- Website and search presence, when available.
- Local community groups and social media.
- Referral activity through existing personal networks.
- Direct outreach to potential B2B customers.

## Customer relationships

- Self-service discovery and requests for individual customers.
- Clear support and issue-handling process.
- Managed support for B2B accounts if/when the B2B offering is validated.
- Human review of flagged or disputed cases.

## Revenue streams — phased

1. **Advertising:** considered after sufficient user density and traffic exist.
2. **Premium Emergency Tier:** considered after sufficient trusted technician supply exists to support urgent requests reliably.
3. **B2B Elite Contracts:** longer-term, higher-value opportunity for vetted technicians serving property managers and facilities-management firms.

## Key activities

- Technician referrals and onboarding.
- Identity, profession, and consent verification.
- Customer discovery and service-request handling.
- Review collection and moderation.
- Reputation and Elite criteria management.
- Product development, maintenance, and customer support.
- B2B discovery and validation.

## Key resources

- Technician and referral database.
- Verification and support processes.
- Product platform and infrastructure.
- Review, reputation, and moderation system.
- Local community and acquisition channels.

## Key partners

- Recommenders/Hunters and local communities.
- Independent technicians.
- Potential property-management and facilities-management organizations.
- Technology and infrastructure providers.

## Cost structure

- Product development and maintenance.
- Hosting, infrastructure, and third-party services.
- Verification and customer-support operations.
- Referral reward payouts.
- Marketing and customer acquisition.
- B2B account support
