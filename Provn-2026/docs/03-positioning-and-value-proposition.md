# Positioning & Value Proposition

## Positioning statement

For people in Egypt who need a dependable technician, PROVN is a referral-based technician network that organizes personal recommendations into a searchable way to discover and evaluate service professionals.

Unlike directories that rely mainly on anonymous listings, PROVN starts with a recommendation from someone who knows the technician, then adds identity and profession verification and real customer feedback.

## Customer-facing value proposition

**Find technicians through trusted recommendations, clear verification, and real customer reviews.**

PROVN helps customers move beyond scattered word-of-mouth by making recommendations easier to discover and compare.

## Why the model is different

- **Referral-first:** a technician can be introduced by someone who personally knows them.
- **Clear verification:** identity, profession, and consent are checked; this is not a skills certification.
- **Useful reviews:** customers rate Speed, Quality, and Behavior separately.
- **Reputation over time:** credibility grows through real service interactions, not simply by creating a profile.

## What PROVN should not claim

- Do not say every technician is skill-certified or guaranteed.
- Do not claim that every recommendation is independently proven to be true.
- Do not claim competitors cannot copy the model or that PROVN has no competitors.
- Do not promise a technician's performance before enough real service evidence exists.
