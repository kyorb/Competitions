# Validation & Pilot Framework

This document separates the proposed pilot design from proven results. All numbers below should be added only after the team has approved and resourced the pilot.

## 1. Early adopter hypotheses

Potential early customer groups:
- Homeowners and families who arrange maintenance services.
- Residents in the proposed initial districts: Maadi, Nasr City, and Helwan.
- People who currently rely on personal recommendations to find technicians.

Potential technician group:
- Independent technicians who are comfortable being recommended, contacted for verification, and reviewed after real jobs.

## 2. Suggested pilot scope

Start in a limited geographic area and test the full product loop:

**Recommend → Verify → Discover → Request → Complete Service → Review → Reward eligibility**

## 3. What to validate

### Customer questions
- Do users understand the difference between a personal recommendation and a random listing?
- Does verification information increase confidence?
- Can users compare technicians using Speed, Quality, and Behavior?
- Do users successfully complete a service request through the product?

### Technician questions
- Are technicians willing to be recommended and contacted?
- Do they understand and consent to the verification process?
- Does the platform help them reach customers beyond their existing network?
- Are they willing to receive and respond to customer reviews?

### Hunter questions
- Do people understand the referral role?
- Do reward conditions encourage useful referrals rather than spam?
- Can the team verify that reward eligibility conditions have actually been met?

### B2B questions
- Do property managers or facilities-management teams have a recurring need that PROVN can serve?
- What coverage, response-time, quality, and reporting requirements would they need?
- Would they consider a pilot with a sufficiently reliable technician pool?

## 4. Suggested metrics

- **Referral completion rate:** share of submitted referrals that complete required verification steps.
- **Service completion rate:** share of accepted requests that result in a confirmed completed service.
- **Review rate:** share of completed services that receive a customer review.
- **Repeat usage:** share of customers who return and make another request within a defined period.
- **Issue/complaint rate:** share of completed services associated with a reported issue.
- **Reward eligibility and redemption:** number of referrals that meet the published reward conditions and the share of eligible rewards redeemed.

## 5. Verification boundary

PROVN verification should clearly cover:
1. Technician identity.
2. Profession/trade information.
3. Consent to be listed and contacted.

## 6. Elite status

Elite should be earned, not purchased. Publish objective criteria before assigning the status. Potential criteria may include verified identity/trade, completed services, review quality, reliability, and complaint handling.
