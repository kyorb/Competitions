# TalkMate
### Breaking Language Barriers

> Watch the Demo Video: [Click here to watch the Demo Video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12RqJ2_KlcloMJaTu3EIhwwkPhalqdVgX/view?usp=sharing)

**TalkMate** is a real-time language translation application designed to make communication between people who speak different languages effortless and natural.

Using regular Bluetooth earphones and smartphones, TalkMate allows two or more people to communicate in their native languages without needing to understand each other's language.

Our mission is simple: **Break language barriers and bring people closer together.**

---

## The Problem

Language barriers make everyday communication difficult, whether when traveling, meeting new people, attending international meetings, or communicating with people from different cultures.

Traditional translation tools often interrupt conversations, requiring users to type, switch between screens, or manually translate sentences.

TalkMate aims to eliminate these interruptions by making translation a natural part of the conversation.

## Our Solution

TalkMate provides real-time voice translation, allowing people to speak naturally in their own languages while hearing translated speech through their earphones.

Each participant independently selects their preferred language, ensuring that everyone receives translations in the language they understand.

No special translation hardware is required — just a smartphone and regular Bluetooth earphones.

## Key Features

### 1. Instant Voice Translation
Translate spoken conversations in real time, making face-to-face communication easier and faster.

### 2. Bidirectional Translation
Enable two people speaking different languages to communicate naturally, with translations delivered to each participant in their selected language.

### 3. Multiple Conversation Modes

- **Conversation Mode:** For everyday face-to-face conversations.
- **Meeting Mode:** Designed for group discussions and meetings.
- **Private Chat Mode:** A more personal translation experience.
- **Subtitles Mode:** Displays translated conversations as text.

### 4. Learning Center
Turn conversations into learning opportunities.

- Conversation history.
- Saved vocabulary.
- Interactive quizzes.
- Personalized language practice.

### 5. Offline Translation
Access translation capabilities without an internet connection through the premium experience.

### 6. Personalization
Customize your experience and manage your preferred languages and settings.

## Technology Stack

TalkMate combines mobile development, AI capabilities, and translation technologies to deliver a seamless multilingual communication experience.

- **FlutterFlow:** Cross-platform mobile application development.
- **Google Gemini API:** AI integration for intelligent language-related features and AI-powered experiences.
- **LibreTranslate:** Open-source machine translation service.
- **Bluetooth Earphones:** Audio output for translated speech.
- **Firebase / Supabase:** Potential backend infrastructure for data management and future integrations.

*The technology stack may evolve as development progresses.*
